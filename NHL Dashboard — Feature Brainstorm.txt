## Ideas Backlog

### Fan-focused
- Pinned "my team" view — Penguins front and center, rest of league secondary
- Rivalry tracker — head-to-head record vs a specific team over the season
- Playoff odds / "would we make it if the season ended today" widget

### Data-nerd
- Player-level stats (goals, assists, +/-, TOI) — ties into the drill-down view above
- Advanced stats overlay (Corsi, xG) from MoneyPuck's public CSVs
- Auto-flag teams on a 5+ game win/lose streak as a callout, not just a table column

### Fun / gimmicky
- Give teams a "type" based on playstyle (high-scoring = fire, defensive = steel, etc.)
- Subjective "power rankings" tab layered over the objective stats

### Infra / portfolio-flex
- Email/text alert when the Penguins win (Azure Function, event-driven)
- `/health` endpoint on the API
- Redis caching layer in front of the API to reduce repeated Postgres hits
- Terraform for infra provisioning (per original roadmap)
- Historical season-over-season comparison views

### Design
- Redesign layout with Claude's design help — sidebar for trend chart instead of
  below-the-fold, team logos, "favorite team" pinned card
