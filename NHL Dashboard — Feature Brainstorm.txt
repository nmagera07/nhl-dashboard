# NHL Dashboard — Feature Brainstorm

A running list of ideas for future work. Not commitments, just a parking lot.
Add to this as new ideas come up; check items off or move them to "In Progress"
when you actually start building them.

Last updated: 2026-10-09

---

## In Progress
- **NHL Intelligence as an agent** — tool calling over the app's data and the
  playoff-odds model: standings, schedules, schedule strength, odds history,
  players, "what if they win their next 4", and "what does Detroit need".
  Next: an eval suite (does it pick the right tool; do answers match the
  tools' numbers?), then "why did the odds change?" and a daily digest.
- **Push notifications** — "🔔 Goal alerts" on the My team card: a push for
  every goal in your team's games (either side) and the final score, within
  about a minute. Watched by the nhl-live-notifier job (every minute during
  game hours, inside Azure's free allowance).
- **Season simulator** — "Sim the season" on team pages (linked from the My
  team card): plays out the remaining schedule in the browser with the
  playoff-odds model, one season per click (final record, playoff seed,
  game-by-game strip), plus a 3,000-season points spread.

## Done recently
- **"My team"** — follow a team (★ on its team page, or the picker on
  Scores; remembered in the browser). Scores leads with its card: record,
  division spot, playoff odds, live/next game, last 5 results. A ★ marks it
  in standings and the race table, and Standings opens on its division.

## Ideas Backlog

### Fan-focused
- Push notifications for goals and final scores ("Goal! PIT 2–1, Crosby (5)").
  Works for installed PWAs on Android and iPhone (iOS 16.4+). Needs: a
  permission prompt + saved subscriptions, a backend that watches live games
  (server-side polling + cache), and Web Push sending. Replaces the old
  "email/text alert when the Penguins win" idea.

### Data-nerd
- Auto-flag teams on a 5+ game win/lose streak as a callout, not just a table column
- Contract details on the player page (cap hit, term, expiry, NMC/NTC). No free
  official source: the NHL API has no contract data, and CapFriendly was bought
  and shut down by the Capitals in 2024. Options:
  - Start small: hand-maintained CSV for one team (or favorite players) loaded
    into a `player_contracts` table. Contracts change a few times a year, so
    it's manageable.
  - Paid APIs: PuckPedia or Spotrac (check current pricing and terms)
  - EliteProspects has a commercial API, good for prospects and junior and
    international careers, but not a cap source
  - Avoid scraping: it breaks ToS and fails silently when sites change
  - Fits the existing pattern: new table + ingestion script + weekly schedule
- Historical season-over-season comparison views (team level; player
  season-by-season already exists)

### Fun / gimmicky
- Give teams a "type" based on playstyle (high-scoring = fire, defensive = steel, etc.)
- Subjective "power rankings" tab layered over the objective stats

### Infra / portfolio-flex
- Always-on API (min replicas 1, ~$5-10/month) to skip the ~20s cold start
- Playoff odds: roster-aware preseason ratings (build each team's prior from
  its current players). The one model idea left with real upside; adjusted
  xG, strength of schedule, and goaltending were all tested and didn't help.
- Turn on Sentry in production (already wired into the code; just needs
  `SENTRY_DSN` as a Container App secret). Then drop the last Azure alert and
  take the Azure bill from ~$0.97/month to $0.
- Terraform for infra provisioning (per original roadmap) — would also have
  prevented the leftover-resource surprises (second registry, extra workspace)
- App store version via Capacitor (wraps the PWA). Apple $99/yr, Google $25
  one-time, plus app review. Only if store presence is worth it.
